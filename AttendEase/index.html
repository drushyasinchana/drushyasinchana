⚙️ How to connect your Google Sheets — Step-by-step setup

Follow these steps exactly. You only need to do this once per company account.

Master Control Sheet (Drushyasinchana manages this)
The MASTER sheet at Drushyasinchana's Google account contains all company registrations. Columns are: companyId, companyName, adminEmail, companySheetId, companyScriptUrl, isActive, startDate, endDate.
The adminEmail is what you enter as "Master Control Gmail ID" at login.

Create your company Google Sheet
In your own Google account, create a new Google Sheet. Create 4 tabs named exactly: EMPLOYEES, SITES, ATTENDANCE_YYYY_MM (one per month, e.g. ATTENDANCE_2026_03), and APP_LOGS.
Copy the Sheet ID from the URL: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/[SHEET-ID]/edit

EMPLOYEES sheet columns (row 1 = header)
EMPID | EmpName | Email | Phone | PasswordHash | Site | JoinDate | Status | PhotoURL | Role | EffectiveDate
Add your first admin row. For password, use SHA-256 hash of the password. Role must be ADMIN or EMPLOYEE.

SITES sheet columns
SiteID | SiteName | Latitude | Longitude | Radius | ShiftStart | ShiftEnd | LunchTime | Address | Status
Add at least one site (e.g. SiteID = SITE001, Status = ACTIVE). Radius is in metres.

ATTENDANCE sheet columns (one tab per month)
Date | EMPID | Name | SiteID | InTime | OutTime | Status | LocationStatus | MarkedBy | HalfDay | ManualRemark | Remarks
The Android app writes to this sheet automatically. The portal reads from it.

Deploy a Google Apps Script (Web App) for your company sheet
In your company Sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script. Paste the Apps Script code (provided below). Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app → Execute as: Me → Anyone. Copy the deployment URL and send it to Drushyasinchana to add to the Master Control sheet.

Register your company in Master Control
Send Drushyasinchana: your company name, your Gmail ID (adminEmail), your Sheet ID, and your Apps Script deployment URL. They will add a row in the MASTER sheet. After that, login on this portal will work.

How login works on this portal
You enter your Gmail ID → portal calls the Master Script which checks MASTER sheet → finds your companyScriptUrl → calls your company's Apps Script → that script validates credentials from your EMPLOYEES sheet → returns success + company data → portal loads your dashboard.
